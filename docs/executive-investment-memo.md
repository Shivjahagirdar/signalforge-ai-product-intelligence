- Why this problem: revenue-weighted prioritisation is manual and error-prone (hypothesis - verify in interviews).
- Why this MVP: one upload-to-PRD journey; smallest slice that demonstrates unique-account exposure and explainability.
- Why not competitors: unverified; see research backlog in `competitive-gap-analysis.md`.
- Estimated value: modelled only (see in-app memo and `go-to-market.md`).
- What invalidates it: PMs will not upload feedback exports; incumbents already do ACV-weighted ranking; clustering fails on real text.
- Build next: five discovery interviews, real-data pilot with a design partner, then Zendesk/Salesforce import.
- Deliberately not build: churn prediction, SSO, billing.
