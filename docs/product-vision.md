# Product vision
**From customer noise to capital allocation intelligence.** Roadmaps should be funded by evidence, revenue exposure and effort - with the reasoning visible and the human in control.
North Star (hypothesis, unmeasured): evidence-backed product decisions completed per active product team per month = decisions exported as PRD with >=1 linked ticket / active teams / month. Instrumentation (future): `prd_exported`, `override_applied`, weekly active teams. Do not claim improvement until measured.
Principles: explainable over autonomous; scenario estimates never presented as churn; synthetic data always labelled.
