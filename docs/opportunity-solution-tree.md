# Opportunity-solution tree
**Outcome:** higher share of roadmap spend justified by evidence and revenue linkage.
- Opp 1: High-ACV pain is hidden in low-volume tickets -> unique-account exposure view; ACV-weighted rank.
- Opp 2: Duplicates inflate priority -> exact dedupe + near-duplicate flags.
- Opp 3: Scores are not explainable -> evidence trail, "why A outranks B".
- Opp 4: Assumptions are hidden -> sliders, bear/base/bull, tornado, Monte Carlo.
- Opp 5: PRDs are not traceable -> PRD cites ticket IDs.
**Chosen first:** Opp 1+3 (narrowest valuable slice). Rejected for MVP: churn prediction (needs outcome data), integrations (cost, no differentiation).
