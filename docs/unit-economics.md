## Unit economics (illustrative inputs - replace with measured values)
Assumed: gross margin 80%, blended CAC $1,500, monthly churn 3% -> LTV = ARPA x GM / churn. With the blended ARPA of $409/mo: LTV = 409 x 0.8 / 0.03 = ~$10,907; LTV:CAC ~ 7.3; CAC payback = 1,500 / (409 x 0.8) = ~4.6 months. These are assumptions, not results.

## Channel choice
Lowest-cost credible route to first design partners: GitHub/open-source demo + PM community posts + direct outreach. Validation plan: 5 discovery interviews -> 3 design partners -> 1 paid pilot. Kill condition: fewer than 2 of 5 interviewees say they would pilot with their own CSV.
